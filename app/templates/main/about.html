About Rural Hub

Bridging the digital divide in rural communities.

🎯 Our Mission

Rural Hub aims to empower rural populations by providing a simple, accessible digital platform tailored to their unique economic needs. We focus on sustainability, community trust, and local empowerment.

💡 Why We Built This

Many rural areas lack centralized platforms for selling scrap at fair prices, finding reliable local services, or engaging in traditional barter. Rural Hub digitizes these age-old practices, making them safer, wider-reaching, and more transparent.

👨‍💻 The Team

Built as a project to demonstrate full-stack capabilities, cloud deployment on Vercel, and modern UX design tailored for accessibility and ease of use.
